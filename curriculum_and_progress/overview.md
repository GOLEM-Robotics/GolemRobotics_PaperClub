## Execution statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Shared Core** | Common technical language and research standard for all members. |
| **Active Research Track** | Immediately executable, project-facing research line. |
| **Specialization** | Deeper branch owned by a subset of members. |
| **Optional** | Activated when project scope makes it relevant. |
| **Frontier Watchlist** | Monitored without destabilizing the durable curriculum. |
| **Deferred** | Retained in the knowledge map but not currently scheduled. |
